# Layers and G-code

Layers come from colour: each distinct colour drawn by the project's SVG objects becomes a layer with laser settings, and the settings of a new layer come from the laser palette. This article covers the palette and the paint and layer derivation (roadmap item 4a, Tasks 1 and 2); the project document integration, toolpath and G-code writer are added as they land. Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-01_(02)_layers-gcode.spec.md`.

## Key Takeaways

- **Palette.** `Palette {version, entries}` holds `PaletteEntry {id, name, colour, mode, maxPowerPercent, minPowerPercent, speedMmPerMin, passes, airAssist, lineIntervalMm}`. `LAYER_MODE` is `line` or `fill`. `DEFAULT_PALETTE` is Cut (`#ff0000`, line, 100 %, 300 mm/min, 3 passes), Score (`#0000ff`, line, 30 %, 1500), Fill (`#00a000`, fill, 50 %, 2000) and the built-in Shading entry (`SHADING_ENTRY_ID`, `#000000`, fill, 60 %, 2000). Line interval defaults to 0.1 mm. Entries are validated by `getPaletteEntryErrors`, the palette is stored in localStorage (`palette.storage.ts`), and `getInkscapePaletteText` writes the `.gpl` file for Inkscape. `getNormalizedColour` turns names, short hex and `rgb()` into lowercase `#rrggbb`.
- **Style resolver.** `getResolvedSvgStyles(svgDocument)` returns a `Map<Element, SvgResolvedStyle>` for every element (also inside `defs`, so the geometry code can reuse it for `use`). Precedence, lowest to highest: presentation attribute, `<style>` rule, inline `style`. Rules are simple selectors only (type, `.class`, `#id`, compounds of those, comma lists); weight is id > class > type, and a later rule wins at equal weight. Any other selector and every at-rule is ignored. `!important` is stripped, not honoured.
- **Inheritance.** `fill`, `stroke`, `stroke-width`, `fill-opacity`, `stroke-opacity` and `visibility` fall back to the parent. `opacity` does not inherit but multiplies down the tree (`opacity` on the resolved style is the product along the ancestors). `display: none` hides the whole subtree (`isDisplayed`); `visibility: hidden` hides one element and a child can set `visible` again (`isVisible`). Defaults: fill black, stroke none, stroke width 1. `stop-color` and `stop-opacity` are resolved too (not inherited), so classed gradient stops work.
- **Paints.** `getSvgPaints(svgText)` returns the distinct `SvgPaint {colour, opacity, gradient}` of the drawn elements (`path`, `rect`, `circle`, `ellipse`, `line`, `polyline`, `polygon`). It skips `none`, opacity 0, hidden elements, a stroke of width 0, the fill of a `line`, and elements inside `defs`, `clipPath`, `mask`, `pattern`, `marker` and `symbol`. `use` is not followed here. Opacity is element opacity times fill or stroke opacity, rounded to 3 decimals. Paints are distinct by colour, opacity and gradient.
- **Gradients.** `fill="url(#id)"` resolves to a `linearGradient` or `radialGradient` (`SvgGreyGradient`: stops with offset, colour and opacity, geometry, units). Stops and geometry follow `href` / `xlink:href` inheritance (cycle-safe, at most 16 links). The paint colour is the first stop's colour. A missing reference or a gradient without stops gives no paint.
- **Layers.** `ProjectLayer {colour, enabled, order, settings, paletteEntryId, isShading}`; `LayerSettings` is the palette entry without `id`, `name` and `colour`. `getLayerKey` is `SHADING_LAYER_COLOUR` (`'shading'`, not a colour) for the shading layer and the colour for any other.
- **Grey axis.** `isGreyColour` is true when the largest and smallest channel differ by at most 10 (`GREY_AXIS_TOLERANCE`); `getColourLightness` is `(r + g + b) / (3 * 255)`.
- **Derivation.** `getDerivedLayers(objectsPaints, palette, existingLayers)` keeps every existing layer unchanged and adds a layer for each key without one. `getPaintLayerKey`: greys and gradients with only grey stops go to the shading layer; a gradient with a colour stop goes to its first stop colour. A palette colour gets its entry's settings, enabled. Any other colour gets the settings of the nearest non-shading entry by Euclidean RGB distance (an unknown colour never borrows the shading settings), `enabled: false`, `paletteEntryId: undefined`. New layers are ordered fill before line, then palette order, then unknown colours, and numbered after the existing layers' highest order.
